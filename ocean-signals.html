The mission

The ocean is connected. Our data should be, too.

A large-scale ocean event may leave different signatures along different coastlines. Our project brings global observations and local records into the same frame so those connections can be explored, measured and communicated.

How do we translate ocean-scale patterns into a clearer understanding of change at the coastline?
